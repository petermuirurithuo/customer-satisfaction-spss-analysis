# Customer Satisfaction Survey Analysis (IBM SPSS)

## Overview
Statistical analysis of a 120 respondent customer satisfaction survey using IBM SPSS Statistics. The project tests four business questions using Independent Samples T Tests and Chi Square tests to identify what drives customer satisfaction and loyalty.

## Dataset
120 customer records with the following variables: Customer ID, Group (A/B), Satisfaction Score (1 to 10), Age, Gender, Location, Purchase History, Support Contacted, Loyalty Level (Low/Medium/High), and Satisfaction Factor.

## Business Questions Tested
1. Does Group A or B have higher satisfaction? (Independent Samples T Test)
2. Does contacting support affect loyalty level? (Chi Square Test)
3. Does purchase history affect satisfaction? (Independent Samples T Test)
4. Which satisfaction factor is linked to loyalty? (Chi Square Test)

## Key Findings
- No significant difference in satisfaction between Group A and Group B, p = .508.
- No significant relationship between support contact and loyalty level, p = .182, though low loyalty customers contacted support more often (55.6 percent versus 35.1 percent for high loyalty customers).
- Satisfaction factor was not significantly linked to loyalty level, p = .495.
- Purchase history comparison could not be completed due to a variable coding issue, flagged for correction.
